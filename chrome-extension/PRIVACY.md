# attestq for Chrome: privacy policy

**What it reads:** only the tab where you click the attestq icon, and only
after you click **Scan & Answer**. It reads the text of the form's questions,
their answer options, and any answers already on the page.

**Where that goes:** only to the attestq server set in the extension's settings,
either by you or by your organisation's administrator. That server belongs to
you or your organisation. The extension's authors run no server and receive
nothing.

**What it stores:**
- **In your browser, until the tab closes:** the questions and drafted answers
  for the page you're working on.
- **In Chrome's settings storage:** your settings (server address, optional
  access token, preferences).

**What it sends back:** after you fill a form, it sends the drafted and the final
text of each filled answer to the same server. This is used to measure how often
drafts are accepted. Your administrator can turn this off by not enabling
feedback on the server.

**What it doesn't do:** no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no sale or
transfer of data, and no access to any other tab or site.
